About Us

About Us

A vibrant, healthy, and resilient continent where poverty and the burden of disease are negligible.

We design programs with communities, not merely for them. Our community service focuses on education, prevention, screening, referral, and practical support that moves people closer to the healthiest choice.

Volunteer, internship, mentorship, school health, outreach, and partnership opportunities are available for people and organizations who want to help advance health literacy and equity.

Vision and Approach to our work

Our vision is a vibrant, healthy, and resilient continent where poverty and the burden of disease are negligible. Our mission is to improve the knowledge and competencies of African communities through education, awareness creation, community prevention, and screening.

OUR CORE VALUES

  1. Knowledge

    Promote knowledge and soft skill development among women and marginalized groups Promote healthier lives through sensitizations and health talks

  2. DIVERSITY AND EQUITY.

    We dedicate our efforts to fighting all forms of abuse and social injustices Encourage dissent and different perspectives through our work.

  3. Confidentiality.

    We protect the data of our stakeholders We only share information after consent has been granted

  4. Integrity and Human Dignity.

    Treat others with respect and dignity We master transparency and accountability
