Privacy Policy

AFRICHEL collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, manage volunteers and partners, deliver programs, and support screened participants with consent.

Health information is treated as confidential and shared only with consent or where required by law. We protect personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information by contacting info@africhel.com.
