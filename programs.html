AFRICHEL

Programs

Focused work in education, health, and community support, designed to build health literacy and practical wellbeing where people live, learn, and gather.

Education

Education Programs

We partner with schools and district education offices to build hygiene, self-care, health awareness, and mentoring into daily school life.

Through mentorship, skills development, and access to professional networks, AFRICHEL helps young people and volunteers build confidence, discover opportunities, and prepare for meaningful work in health, education, and social impact.

We support schools with practical health talks, hygiene education, prevention messaging, and student-friendly activities that make healthy habits easier to understand and practice.

Health

Health Programs

Our health work focuses on education, screening, counselling, and referral support so people can identify risks early and seek care with confidence.

We promote healthier mothers, children, and families through health education, community sensitization, prevention messages, and helpful information for informed maternal and child wellbeing decisions.

We create safe spaces for mental health awareness and support, encourage open conversations, reduce stigma, and share practical resources that help people seek help early.

Free community health education, blood pressure and blood sugar screening, counselling, and referral guidance help people find risks early and connect to care.

Community Support

Community Support Programs

We work with market associations, schools, faith groups, and neighbourhood leaders to bring trusted health information where people already are.

We work toward stronger, more resilient communities. Our wellbeing activities bring together education, volunteer service, advocacy, and community outreach to support vulnerable people.

AFRICHEL brings practical health information into markets, faith spaces, neighbourhoods, and community gatherings through trusted local relationships.

Volunteers support outreaches, school programs, screening days, advocacy activities, and practical community engagement across AFRICHEL programs.
