Who We Are

AFRICHEL, the African Center for Health Equity and Literacy, is a pan-African nonprofit founded in Ghana by nurses, public health officers, and researchers. We build the knowledge and confidence people need to protect their health in markets, schools, neighbourhoods, and homes.

Activities

Through mentorship, skills development, and access to professional networks, AFRICHEL helps young people and volunteers build confidence, discover opportunities, and prepare for meaningful work in health, education, and social impact.

We promote healthier mothers, children, and families. Our activities focus on health education, community sensitization, basic prevention messages, and connecting families with helpful information so they can make informed decisions about maternal and child wellbeing.

We create safe spaces for mental health awareness and support, encourage open conversations, reduce stigma, and share practical resources that help people seek help early and care for one another with dignity.

We work toward stronger, more resilient communities. Our wellbeing activities bring together education, volunteer service, advocacy, and community outreach to support vulnerable people and encourage healthier, safer, and more inclusive everyday lives.

Areas of work

EDUCATION

We partner with schools and district education offices to build hygiene, self-care, and health awareness into daily school life.

HEALTH

Free community health education, blood pressure and blood sugar screening, counselling, and referral help people find risks early.

Community Support

We work with market associations, schools, faith groups, and neighbourhood leaders to bring trusted health information where people already are.

Our core approaches

Across all our programmes we focus on building communities that are resilient, inclusive and accountable. Our three 'core approaches' - social inclusion and gender, resilience, and social inclusion and accountability are fundamental to our volunteering for development approach, ensuring that the people we work with have the capacity to bring about change in their own lives.

Working in partnership with Organisations

We believe progress is only possible when we work together.
Partnerships lie at the root of all the positive change we create. Together, we work on programme development and delivery, research, fundraising and influencing decision-makers.

Influencing and advocacy

We influence policy at national, regional and international levels. We connect decision makers to the experiences of our volunteers, partners and marginalised people.
Our work around the world, amplifying the voices of the poorest, is making change happen.
