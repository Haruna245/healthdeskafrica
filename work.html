Our Work

AFRICHEL works across the prevention continuum: we stop disease before it starts, find it early when it does start, and keep people connected to care and to each other.

School Health Program

Health habits form in childhood. We partner with schools to build hygiene and self-care into daily school life.

Community Screening & Referral

Free screening outreaches in markets and communities with counselling and referral into real care.

Health Education & Awareness

Plain-language health talks, durbars, digital content, and campaigns in places people already gather.

Wellbeing & Resilience

Mental health awareness, maternal and child health education, mentorship, and community events.
