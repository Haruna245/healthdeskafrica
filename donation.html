Support Our Work

Every contribution becomes a screening, a school visit, or a health talk in a community that would otherwise go without.

GHS 100

Screens a family of four for high blood pressure and blood sugar.

GHS 500

Supplies hygiene materials and health education for one classroom.

GHS 2,000

Funds a full market outreach day with consumables and volunteer support.

GHS 5,000

Enrols one school in the School Health Program for a full academic year.

Accountability: Fund disbursement follows a strict approval process. A committee reviews every expenditure proposal, and final allocations require approval by the General Assembly.
